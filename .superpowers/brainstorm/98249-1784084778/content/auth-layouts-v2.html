3 — Liquid Orb

A living glass orb floats over midnight — tap "Continue" and the orb itself morphs into the email field, then splits into OTP digits, glowing green when verified. One continuous liquid animation instead of screens. Pure Liquid-Glass theatre.

Combos: e.g. Liquid Orb entrance (3) + Biometric Vault (1) for returning users is a killer pairing. KYC screen is last.
